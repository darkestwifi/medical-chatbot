# Project notes

## Aim
Build a bilingual Hindi/English mental-wellness chatbot demonstration.

## Suggested future modules
- User registration and login
- Mood check-in and mood-history chart
- Guided breathing and grounding activities
- Speech input/output
- Privacy notice and consent

## Important limitations
Do not present generated responses as medical advice. Do not claim that phrase matching or an LLM can reliably detect suicide risk or diagnose a condition. Add human review and professional guidance before any real-world deployment.
