GET TO KNOW US

About MindCare AI 💜

A friendly mental wellness companion designed to encourage emotional awareness, self-reflection, and positive habits.

🌿 Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday emotional support and wellness tools easier to access. We want to encourage people to express their feelings, reflect on their experiences, and take small steps towards their well-being.

✨ Our Vision

We envision a world where conversations about mental wellness feel more comfortable and accessible, and where everyone feels encouraged to take care of their emotional well-being.

WHAT WE OFFER

Features of MindCare AI

💬

AI Wellness Chat

Chat with an AI companion that offers supportive conversations and helpful reflections.

🎙️

Voice Chat

📊

Mood Tracking

Record your daily moods and observe your emotional patterns over time.

📔

🔐

Personal Accounts

OUR TECHNOLOGY

How MindCare AI Works

MindCare AI combines a Flask-powered web application, an AI language model, and an SQLite database to provide interactive wellness features.
